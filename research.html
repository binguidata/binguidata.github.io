🅿️

Sensor-Less Parking

Real-time spot-occupancy management in managed lots without installing per-spot hardware sensors — state is derived from a JWT-authenticated reservation and check-in flow, with automatic spot allocation and email reminders for upcoming bookings. Implements the non-sensor parking concept of Yang & Qian (CMU CEE) on a Django REST + React stack; deployed at the CMU Morewood lot and structured to scale.

Sensor-Less Occupancy Reservation Flow CMU Morewood
⛰️

Landslide Early Warning System (Maryland DOT)

Developed precipitation-based landslide risk assessment and alert dashboard for Maryland State Highway Administration. Integrates real-time weather data, soil moisture prediction, and geospatial slope analysis for statewide monitoring and early warning.

Risk Assessment Open-Meteo API GIS Integration
